# R08.3a — Trap & Drill
Twenty original patches, two per WL EXPANSION instrument. Trap design emphasizes longer sub sustain, rounded attacks, nocturnal FM and spacious layers. Drill design emphasizes shorter gates, distorted bass, metallic transients and brass stabs. Names indicate intended use, not emulations or a guarantee of genre authenticity. Existing three factory patches per instrument retain their indices. All new patches appear in native preset selectors and pass through the existing MIDI/preview/render system. A browser gate renders all fifty patches, checks finite samples, peak and non-silence, and compares normalized temporal energy profiles for each new genre pair. This does not substitute for listening or a completed composition. CI must pass before merge.
