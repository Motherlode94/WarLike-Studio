# R07.2f — VOX LAB and VOX LAB PRO selected hardware suite
Five redesigned processors: TUNE (5 controls), CLEAN (6), LEVEL (6), AIR EQ (7), AMBIENCE (6). All 25 factory presets and the existing local personal bank are available on track and Master. AIR EQ and AMBIENCE retain their VOX LAB PRO catalog membership. This certification does not cover the other VOX LAB PRO processors.
The order TUNE → CLEAN → LEVEL → AIR EQ → AMBIENCE is an example, not a forced chain. TUNE is monophonic and should normally be used on an isolated vocal track, not a polyphonic master mix.
Shared preset infrastructure preserves per-module state. CI verifies five simultaneous Master surfaces, portable save and insert order, personal preset reuse on a track, all four themes and mobile. Existing isolated audio/DSP gates retained. Real microphone recording and manual listening are not certified by this check.

## R07.2g–i extension
MIC COLOR, WARM and DOUBLER bring the redesigned family to eight native modules, 46 persisted controls and 40 factory presets. DOUBLER has two physical modulated delay branches; the legacy Voices control scales branch gain and saturates at two, and Detune controls delay-modulation depth, not a fixed pitch offset. Existing project keys and presets remain compatible. Remaining speculative VOX LAB PRO modules still need DSP development.
