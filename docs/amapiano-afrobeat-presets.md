# R08.3b — Amapiano & Afrobeat
20 additional original patches, two per WL EXPANSION instrument. Amapiano targets descending sub pitches, resonant modeled drums, soft FM keys and wider sustained layers; Afrobeat targets tighter bass, horn stabs, offbeat organ and plucked counterpoints. Log drum and talking drum patches are synthetic approximations, not recorded instruments. Existing preset indices remain unchanged. The catalog now contains 535 total presets, including 70 WL EXPANSION patches. Browser tests render all 70 and compare normalized envelope profiles within each genre pair. Genre suitability requires listening and arrangement validation. No DSP changes.
