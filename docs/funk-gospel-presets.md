# R08.3d — Funk & Gospel
Twenty original presets using the ten WL EXPANSION instruments. Funk targets short attacks, clipped drawbars, syncopated bass, metallic percussion, brass hits and FM clavinet-like tones. Gospel targets sustained bass foundations, fuller additive organ, synthetic choir, strings and electric keys. They are original synthetic starting points, not recorded instruments or proprietary emulations. Previous factory indices remain unchanged. Total Studio preset count: 575; expansion count: 110. The native browser gate renders every expansion patch and compares normalized temporal energy profiles of each genre pair. Musical suitability requires listening and arrangement validation. No DSP changes.
